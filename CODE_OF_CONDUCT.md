# Code of Conduct

Be respectful. This is a small hobby project for people who enjoy keeping old devices alive.

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Stay on topic in issues and pull requests.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- No spam, and no requests for or sharing of other people's private data.
- Do not ask for help with piracy, account theft, or abuse of Telegram.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments and close issues that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.
Report problems by opening a private security advisory or contacting the maintainers through the repository.
